# Draft prior-publication disclosure and cover-letter material

Prepared for author review. This document has not been sent to an editor or journal.

Dear Editor,

Please consider the accompanying manuscript, “Multimodal Deep Learning for Real-Time Glucose Prediction with Zero/Few-Shot On-Premises LLM Explainability: External Validation on an Independent Adolescent Cohort,” as a retrospective
methods and empirical evaluation study. The manuscript distinguishes forecasting performance,
model attribution and source-relative language-output consistency. It reports negative and
uncertain findings alongside favorable results and does not claim clinical deployment safety.

We disclose the related journal article by Hossain and Kim, “Multimodal Deep Learning for
Real-Time Glucose Prediction with LLM Explainability,” Journal of the Korean Institute of
Convergence Signal Processing, 2026, volume 27, pages 143–150,
https://doi.org/10.23087/jkicsp.2026.27.3.006. This is a prior journal publication, not a conference
abstract for which a conference-extension exception should be assumed.

The new manuscript uses newly fitted reduced-input models, sensor-aware observed-target
manifests, shared patient-disjoint selection, three seeds, explicit baseline and ablation
comparisons, participant-level inference, genuine held-out TreeSHAP and a separately audited
generation contract. A separately labelled extension tests frozen newly trained glucose-only models on AZT1D, evaluates Chronos-2 on matched primary origins, expands RF inner validation and measures sequential warm processing latency. These additions do not constitute a clinical trial or authenticate historical weights. It does not reuse the earlier artwork or present its reported OhioT1DM
performance as independently reproduced. The previous six-modal checkpoints, reconstructed
signals and evaluation outputs were unavailable in the repository reviewed for this revision.
The earlier paper's numerical claims therefore cannot be verified by this new analysis.

The manuscript explains this limitation and the relationship between the two studies. The
authors should provide the earlier publication with the submission and invite editorial
assessment of overlap and originality. Any correction warranted in the earlier publication
must be addressed with its publisher; changing this manuscript cannot retrospectively validate
that publication.

Code, aggregate results, source checksums, prompts, detector challenges and reproduction
instructions are prepared as a supplementary evidence archive. Raw participant records remain
with their original providers. Substantive AI assistance is disclosed; AI systems are not listed
as authors.

Before this cover letter is used, the human authors must confirm its factual contents, the
selected journal, manuscript approval, authorship/funding/conflict declarations, applicable
secondary-data ethics determination and the absence of simultaneous submission. These
confirmations have not been fabricated in this draft.
